What Is Them's Fightin' Herds?

Them's Fightin' Herds (or "TFH") is a traditional 2D fighter, much like Mortal Kombat or Skullgirls. What originally started as a dynamic cast of six characters soon grew to almost double that by the end of development. It had a dynamic music system and even a story mode, but neither of these ended up truly finished. This is, of course, thanks to the publishing deal they got in January 2022.

Context Behind The Game

In it's simplest terms, TFH was a game developed by bronies. Exactly nine of them. It's the successor to a fangame of My Little Pony, titled Fighting is Magic.
